Weekly OT Rules: Each payroll week runs Saturday–Friday. Weekday OT is calculated Monday–Friday after 40.0 worked hours. Saturday OT is finalized after the weekdays are complete: weekday OT of 8h+ = all Saturday 2x; 7h = first 1h at 1.5x; 6h = first 2h at 1.5x; 5h = first 3h at 1.5x; 4h or less = first 4h at 1.5x; remaining Saturday hours are 2x. Sunday is always 2x. Saturday and Sunday show Waiting to End Week until all five weekday entries are saved. 0.5h unpaid lunch applies only when a weekday shift exceeds 4.0h; Saturday keeps its existing 0.5h lunch deduction when over 4h.
